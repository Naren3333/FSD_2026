# ADR 0021: Class-insights copilot

Status: accepted 2026-11-21. The project owner asked for workflow 5 of `docs/architecture/agentic-workflows.md`, the teacher class-insights copilot, to be built: a teacher asks one question about a class and gets one checked answer, without searching teaching material.

## Context

The Class dashboard shows a topic × student heatmap, and Learning performance interprets one student at a time (ADR 0014). Nothing answers class-level questions such as "who is struggling with recursion, and what should I reteach?". Answering one means choosing which topics, questions and students to look at, the same tool-using shape as the interpretation agent. The ten-application rule rules out a new service, and the interpretation agent already reads every service this needs.

## Decision

**The copilot is a second route in `student-performance-agent`, on the interpretation agent's loop.**

- `POST /v1/class_insights` takes a classroom, a question (1–500 characters) and up to three earlier questions. Only the classroom's teacher may call it (checked at Identity); students and service accounts get 403. It is published through the existing Kong route `/api/performance-agent` and called with the teacher's own token. Kong, Keycloak and Compose need no change.
- The turn loop of ADR 0014 moves into the service's own `loop.py`: the final-turn rule, the report reserve, the forced report after an unusable or failed turn, side-by-side tools with repeats refused, the step trace and the request budget. Both agents plug in their own workspace, tools, prompts and checks. Both users are in one service, so the loop stays there and is not moved into `learning_transport`. Interpretation's behaviour is unchanged, except that its step reasons show words in place of references (below).
- It is stateless. A follow-up resends earlier questions only. They reach the model as context, never as evidence, and the evidence is read again for every question. Nothing the browser sends is trusted as evidence.
- It opens with the class's released topic metrics from Analytics (the first 200 students in roster order, with the population, compared count and partial flag), the roster's names from Identity (up to 500) and the topic names from Curriculum. A class with no released evidence gets `422 NO_EVIDENCE` without a model call.
- Then up to five model turns under the interpretation agent's settings (`PERFORMANCE_MAX_TURNS`, `PERFORMANCE_DEADLINE`, `PERFORMANCE_BUDGET`). Each is one structured ai-core `generate` call as the `student-performance-agent` service account, billed to the teacher (ADR 0003), under the schema names `class_insights.turn` and `class_insights.report`. A turn calls up to three read-only tools or reports:
  - `topic_detail` (topic) lists every student's released evidence on the topic, from the opening read.
  - `topic_questions` (topic) reads the class's finalized evaluations from Grading (newest first, up to five pages of 100) once per request, shared by parallel calls, and the teacher's view of the assessments. For each question on the topic it shows how many students answered and lost marks, the mean marks lost, the expected answer and the answers that lost marks. It says `older_results_not_read` when the five pages left older evaluations unread, which may be finalized or drafts; drafts count toward those pages.
  - `student_results` (student) shows one student's topic figures and newest finalized results (up to 20, as interpretation reads them), marks only, and says `older_results_not_read` when grading has older evaluations it did not read, which may be finalized or drafts. It reads only the students the class figures cover: the report schema offers only their references, and any other student is refused.
  - `read_answers` (a question or a result, topic optional with a result) shows the answers, lost marks first, at most eight.
  - `prerequisites` (topic) reads a topic's prerequisites from Curriculum, with the class's figures on each.
- Each tool turn lists `suggested_checks` not yet made: `topic_questions` on every topic at least two students struggle with, then `read_answers` on that topic's question that lost the most students marks. The instructions ask for a struggling group for every topic with struggling students when the question asks who is struggling. They also ask that each text say what the evidence shows (how many struggle, the class's share of the marks, which questions lost marks and what the answers got wrong), and that it never write references, which the teacher cannot read. Added 2026-11-21, after the first eval run left out a student weak on every topic and wrote "listed as struggling" texts.
- The model refers to students, topics, questions, results and answers by short references (`S1`, `T1`, `Q1`, `R1`, `E1`). **Names never reach the model.** Identity's names stay in the agent and are added to the response after the checks, for the cited students only. The question is the teacher's own text and is sent as given.
- The agent does not search Content or call the interpretation agent. Each student in the answer links to Learning performance, where the teacher can ask for an interpretation.

**The answer is checked against what the agent gathered; failures are dropped and counted, never repaired.**

- A struggling group must name a shown topic. Each student in it must have enough released evidence on that topic (Analytics' `observed` status) and either the needs-practice flag or under 60% of the marks. A student with too little evidence (`INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE`) or without that evidence (`NOT_SUPPORTED`) is dropped, and a group left with nobody goes too.
- An observation must cite a shown topic or answer, and a student it names must have evidence on a cited topic or own a cited answer. A possible misconception may cite only answers that lost marks and that the model read with `read_answers`. A reteach item must name a shown topic, and its cited answers must have been shown.
- Every percentage in a struggling, observation, misconception or reteach text ("73%", "73 percent") must be, within 0.5 percentage points, a share the model was shown about what the item cites. Those are, for each topic the item cites (a group's or reteach item's topic, an observation's topics, and the topics of its cited answers), with the prerequisites `prerequisites` showed for them:
  - the class's share of the marks, and the share of the students with evidence on the topic who struggle;
  - each cited student's (and each cited answer's student's) share of the marks and recent marks share on it;
  - for each question row `topic_questions` showed on it, the share of the students who answered that lost marks and the mean marks lost over the possible marks, and the complement of each;
  - each cited answer's own marks.
  A text with any other percentage is dropped and counted as `UNSUPPORTED_FIGURE`, never rewritten; a struggling group with one is dropped whole. The instructions ask for percentages from those figures only, rounded to whole numbers. Added 2026-11-21, so the answer's figures are checked rather than trusted.
- A text that writes a reference the agent gave out (`S14`, `T2`, `Q5`, `R1`, `E3`) is dropped and counted as `REFERENCE_IN_TEXT`: the teacher sees names beside a statement, never references. Text that only looks like one, such as an assessment titled "T9 quiz" in a four-topic class, is kept. The instructions ask the model to list the students a statement is about and write "one student" or "these students". Added 2026-11-21, after the eval's 14-student class drew "S14 is the only student…" in three runs out of three.
- The step trace shows names too: a step's target is a student's name, a topic's name or an assessment's title, and each reference the model wrote in a step's reason is replaced with its words (a student's name, a topic's name, "a question in Loops quiz", "Ada Lovelace's answer in Loops quiz"). The interpretation agent's reasons are rewritten the same way. Reasons are shown as the model wrote them otherwise.
- At most six struggling groups, five observations, three misconceptions and four reteach items are kept, each text at most 500 characters. When nothing survives, the request fails with `422 INSIGHTS_REJECTED`, the reasons logged, or with `422 NOTHING_TO_ANSWER` when the model reported nothing at all, so nothing was dropped. The response carries the cited answers (with the student, assessment, prompt and marks), the cited students' names, every tool step, the model turns, what was dropped by reason and the class scope. It is marked `requires_teacher_review`, and nothing is saved.

## Consequences

- A question costs up to five model calls, each counted against the teacher's daily allowance. `scripts/eval/run_class_insights.py` measures them on two scripted classes, the larger with borderline and injected students, and can repeat each case to measure how stable the answers are (`docs/development/operations.md`, Agent evals).
- Without provider keys the request answers `503 AI_PROVIDER_NOT_CONFIGURED`.
- The opening and topic figures come from released grades, while the question tools show finalized ones, so with manual release the two can differ until the teacher releases them. The model is told which is which.
- A class over 200 students is answered from its first 200 in roster order. The scope says so, and the model is told not to generalize from it.
- Not built: saved threads, searching teaching material, running the interpretation agent from inside the copilot, and looking a student up by name.
